After some searching online I found a product that started out on
[Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/834664305/clug-cycle-storage-thats-like-a-hug-for-your-bike), called
Clug. The product was launched this year, and the other day I found a distributor that had brought it to Norway.

Clug currently comes in three versions:

- **Roadie** (23 mm to 32 mm)
- **Hybrid** (32 mm to 42 mm)
- **MTB** (1.75″ to 2.5″)

I bought 1 × Roadie and 2 × MTB.

## Mounting

The product is very easy to mount on the wall. All you need is two screws into the wall (included in all packs), then
click on the outer part, and the mount is in place.

Note: for plasterboard or concrete walls you need to get wall plugs yourself for the MTB pack. (Only included in the
Roadie pack.)

The MTB pack also comes with a sticky black pad that is glued to the wall, so the lower tyre can rest against the wall
without leaving marks.

## Experience

The bikes in the MTB mount stand steadily upright without problems, even with the handlebar turned to the side. The
Roadie mount seemed a bit wide for my road bike with the handlebar turned to the side. The bike then tipped slightly
because of the weight of the handlebar. I therefore turned the handlebar back to its normal position, and the bike
then stood upright and stable.
