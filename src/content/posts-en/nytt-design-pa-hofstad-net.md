Hofstad.NET had barely been touched since 2020. The site ran on WordPress with a web host, and most of the work went
into updates, not into writing.

Now it has been rebuilt as a static site. That means there is no admin panel to log in to and no database to maintain.
The posts are plain text files, and the site is built and published automatically when I save a change.

## What is new

- The front page opens with a photo from the archive and has the live map one click away.
- The links to Instagram, Snapchat, YouTube, LiveTrack24 and pgpilot are gathered in one place.
- The posts are sorted into five areas: In the air, Out in nature, Under the surface, Behind the camera and In front of
  the screen.
- The site has light and dark mode, and works just as well on mobile as on a computer.

The old posts have come along. Links from the old blog point to the right place.

## Next

The plan is to use the site a bit more than before. First and foremost it should be the place where you find me, and
the posts will come when there is something worth writing down.
