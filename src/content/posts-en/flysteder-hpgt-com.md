The first question before a flight is always the same: where is the wind coming from, and which site suits it?
[flysteder.hpgt.com](https://flysteder.hpgt.com) is made to answer that at a glance.

The site shows the club's flying sites on a map of Harstad, Kvæfjord and the surrounding area. I put it together for
the club as an addition to [hpgt.com](https://hpgt.com).

## How it works

- Each site has a marker on the map showing which wind directions it works for.
- The list next to it shows altitude, wind directions and today's wind with gusts for each site.
- The timeline at the bottom lets you scroll forward in the forecast, so you can see when during the day it may work.
- You can search by name or municipality and filter the list.
- On mobile the map is at the top and the list below.

The map data comes from the Norwegian Mapping Authority (Kartverket) and OpenStreetMap.

## Remember

The site helps you choose a site, it does not give the final answer. The forecast can be wrong, and the conditions at
launch decide. Always check for yourself before you fly.
