Harstad Hang and Paraglider Club has a new website at [hpgt.com](https://hpgt.com). I put it together for the club,
and it replaces the old site.

The goal was that people who are curious about flying quickly find their way in, and that those of us who already fly
find what we need without searching.

## What you will find

- **Courses:** what it takes to get started, from the first exercises on the ground.
- **Tandem:** for those who want to try a flight together with a pilot.
- **Events and news:** what is happening in the club, with an activity calendar.
- **For pilots:** practical information for members and visiting pilots.
- **Flying sites:** a link to the map of our flying sites.

The site is available in Norwegian and English, has light and dark mode and is built to work well on mobile. The
photos come from the club's own photo collection.

If you have suggestions for something that is missing or should be fixed, just let me know.
